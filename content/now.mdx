## Work

Full-time engineering on AI products, mostly the unglamorous parts that decide whether people keep using a feature: grounding answers in real data, evals, streaming UX, and the plumbing between data and UI. I can't write about the specifics, so what shows up here is the general technique, rebuilt on my own projects.

## Building

- **This site.** Next.js, MDX and a small set of diagram components, so every post can carry its own illustrations.
- **A content machine for myself.** It turns a ticket into a draft post, checks it against my own confidentiality rules, and waits for my approval. It never posts on its own.
- **My personal ops assistant.** It reads my chats read-only, proposes tickets, and drafts my standups. More on that [in the case study](/work/inbox-assistant).
